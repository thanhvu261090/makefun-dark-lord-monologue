Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb): write a monologue of about 100 words, spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. The tiredness lives in the voice, the rhythm, and the small deflections — never a plain statement that the villain is weary — and the speaker is still a villain, with a scheme and a history.

## The entry

> Heroes. You come here, torch in hand, monologue ready — as if I have not heard this exact speech four hundred times. Very well. The crown is behind the door on your left. The curse lifts at dawn, the kingdom sighs, everyone claps. Take it. Honestly, take it. I have ruled this mountain for three centuries and all it taught me is that empires are just paperwork with better lighting. You want a final battle? I want a chair. Sit. We can sign the surrender in the morning, after the ravens stop screaming. Or don't. Either way, I am going to bed.

102 words, 545 characters.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network) holds the single fixed entry string, validates it against the bounty gate — UTF-8 text, 1–600 characters, not only whitespace — then writes it exactly as-is, with no trailing newline, to `/output/entry.txt`.

```bash
python3 main.py            # writes /output/entry.txt
python3 main.py --self-test
```
